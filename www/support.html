Support

How do I play?

You and the computer rival take turns placing pieces on a 3×3 grid: your car against their tyres. Get three in a row across, down, or diagonally to win. If the grid fills with no winner, it's a photo finish (a draw).

Which rival should I pick?

  • Rookie is forgiving and great for kids and first-timers.
  • Pro wins and blocks well, but you can beat it by making two threats at once.
  • Champion plays perfectly and can't be beaten. A draw is the best possible result.

How are points scored?

A win is worth 25 points, a draw 10 and a loss 0. Your season total is saved on your device.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap Sound on at the bottom of the garage screen, or the speaker button during a race.

How do I reset my points?

Tap Reset season at the bottom of the garage screen.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app works fully offline.
