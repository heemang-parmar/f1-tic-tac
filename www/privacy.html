Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Grid Tac Toe does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app stores

The game saves your settings (car colour, rival difficulty, who starts, sound on/off) and your season results (points, wins, draws, losses) only on your own device, using the device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or tapping Reset season, removes it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or personal details are requested.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos are accessed.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • The game runs fully offline and makes no network requests during play.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because no personal information is collected from anyone, no personal information is collected from children.

Website version

If you play in a web browser, the website is hosted by Netlify, which, like any web host, processes standard technical request data (such as IP address) to deliver the pages. We do not use cookies, analytics, or trackers on the site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
