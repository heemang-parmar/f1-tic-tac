How to play

  1. Your car and your rival's tyres take turns on a 3×3 grid.
  2. Get three in a row across, down or diagonally to win the race.
  3. Fill the grid with no winner and it's a photo finish (a draw).
  4. Wins are worth 25 points and draws 10. Build your season total.
  5. Rookie is forgiving, Pro can be outfoxed, and the Champion never loses. A draw against them is a great result.
